# AURO landing page brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The client asked for "a landing page at the highest level you can, not banal, professional, with typography, using motion design" and had already delegated naming, logo and branding ("תמציא שם, לוגו והכל"). No interview answers were given for the eight topics below; every answer is an authored decision, marked as such. Facts come from the client's brief (gold-trading WhatsApp community; shares trade ideas, signals and market analysis; learning together; discipline and responsibility; trading carries risk). Explicit client rules: no personal names anywhere, a more modern Hebrew font than the condensed Noto headline.

## The eight topics (authored)

1. **Vibe:** quiet confidence, gold under studio light, a trading desk at night, together. References: the moment two magnets snap together; a bullion vault's single spotlight; a Bloomberg terminal's level lines.
2. **Journey:** the trader alone, then the same moments done together, then what a real trade idea looks like, then how the group behaves, then the two halves become one and you join.
3. **Energy curve:** calm open, a cooler and plainer middle, one very quiet act, then the largest movement on the page at the close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see the curve and the peak below.
5. **The thing no other site does:** the screen is split by a price level; the gold bar from the brand mark floats alone on one side and, at the end, crosses the level and locks onto the peak on the other side, completing the logo.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal:** premium-minimal is earned here (gold, money, trust), pushed toward *dense* only inside the signal card, which behaves like real trading UI.
7. **One world or distinct scenes:** distinct scenes held inside one split frame. Not a worldflight.
8. **Assets:** the AURO brand system (Ingot A symbol, wordmark, 3D gold renders made with three.js, colours, banners). No photography. The world is rendered 3D gold, not clay.

## Grammar

**Split stage.** The page is an argument with two sides: trading gold alone, or together. The brand's own tagline is that argument ("סוחרים בזהב. צומחים ביחד."). Why the other seven lost: filmic one-shot carries no structure the brief needs; chaptered editorial is for long-form method we do not have; live surface would need a fake WhatsApp chat with invented member messages; continuous world has no geography; typographic poster wastes the 3D mark; gallery has no range of products; rhythmic cutlist is the wrong energy for trust and money.

## Journey (beats)

1. Recognition: you trade gold, and you do it alone.
2. Honesty: the same four moments, alone and together.
3. Clarity: what a real trade idea looks like (entry, stop, targets, reason).
4. Trust: how the group behaves (five rules).
5. Commitment: join.

## Feeling curve

1. **Recognition** — the two halves of the tagline on two sides of a level line; the gold bar hangs alone and slightly off balance on the right.
2. **Honest unease** — four plain rows; the alone side names habits the reader has, without mockery.
3. **Clarity** — a shouted message on the right, and on the left a structured signal card wipes in while its chart line draws itself.
4. **Calm** (authored quiet before the peak) — small type, five rules, the alone side reduced to one line. Nothing moves except text arriving.
5. **Resolve (the peak)** — the level line sweeps to the edge, the bar crosses and locks onto the peak, the A completes, one screen, one action.

## The peak

> "the gold bar that was floating on its own slid across the screen and clicked onto the peak, and the two halves of the page became one"

Lives in act 5. It gets the largest span (2.6 viewport-heights), the live 3D budget, and the quiet act before it.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where a lone gold bar crosses the line in the middle of the screen and locks onto the peak, and the whole page turns into one.

## Authored silence

Act 4 is deliberately quiet: no 3D, no reveal, no pinned travel. It is not dead scroll.

## Constraints carried from the brand and the client

- No personal names. No hit-rate or performance numbers. No invented statistics or counters. No testimonials.
- Every trade-idea number on the page is an example and is labelled "דוגמה".
- Risk disclaimer on the page: "מסחר בזהב כרוך בסיכון. התוכן בקהילה אינו ייעוץ השקעות ואינו מבטיח רווח."
- Hebrew, RTL. Heebo (light/bold contrast) for Hebrew, Archivo Expanded only for the Latin wordmark and Latin labels, Geist Mono only for prices and tickers.
- One CTA label everywhere: "הצטרפות לקהילה". The WhatsApp invite link is not known yet; it lives in one constant.
